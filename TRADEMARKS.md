# GitTree Brand and Trademark Policy

This policy explains how the GitTree name, logo, icon and visual identity may be used with the GitTree source code.

## Source code and commercial use

The GitTree source code is distributed under the MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE). The source license permits commercial use, paid distributions and forks, subject to its terms.

The official GitTree project intends to keep its own builds free of charge. This statement is a project policy and does not modify the rights granted by the source-code license.

## GitTree name and logo

The name “GitTree”, its former “GitTree Minimal” name, the GitTree icon, logos and associated visual identity identify the official project. They are not licensed for use merely because the source code is available under the MIT license.

You may use the name “GitTree” to refer accurately to the unmodified upstream project, its source code or its official releases. You may not:

- name a modified or redistributed product “GitTree” or use its former “GitTree Minimal” name;
- use the GitTree icon or logo as the primary brand of a fork or paid distribution;
- imply that a fork, package, service or commercial distribution is official, sponsored or supported by GitTree;
- use GitTree branding in a way that could confuse users about the source of a build.

Forks and redistributions should choose a distinct name, icon and visual identity while preserving the required copyright and license notices.

## Contributions and permission

Contributing code to GitTree does not transfer ownership of the GitTree trademarks. Permission to use the GitTree name or artwork outside the cases above must be requested from the project owner.

This policy is separate from the source-code license. It is intended to protect users from confusing or misleading branding, not to prevent legitimate references to the upstream project.
